## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer** based in **Curitiba, PR, Brazil** 🇧🇷, where technology meets the law.

- 🔭 Currently building **LawTech** and **smart-city** solutions at **SIRB Desenvolvimento | ICI — Instituto das Cidades Inteligentes**
- 💼 **15+ years** writing software — from Pascal desktop apps to modern **C# / .NET** APIs (with a past life in Java, PHP and C++)
- 🛡️ Deep experience with **judicial systems**, **digital signature validation** and **LGPD-compliant** data anonymization (Brazil's GDPR)
- 🌱 Currently exploring **Rust**, **Python** and **AI/ML**
- 👯 Open to collaborate on **Open Source** projects and **content creation**
- 🎸 Fun fact: when I'm not coding, I'm playing **guitar** or leveling up in **games**
